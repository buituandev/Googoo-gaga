Goo-goo ga-ga is an intelligent, open-source AI translator powered by Google Gemini and offline on-device language detection with Lingua.

Features:
- Direct Translation with screen-optimized layout formatting.
- Deep Linguistic & Cultural Insights: Key terms, slang, idioms, and cultural context breakdown.
- Subtitle & Long Text Translator: Translate SRT/VTT subtitle files with interactive synchronized player.
- Instant System Text Selection: Translate highlighted text directly across any app via Android Process Text.
- 100% Privacy Focused: Your API key is stored strictly on your local device.
